# Executive Summary — Olist Product Analytics

**1. All-time repeat rate: ~3.1%.** Only 3.1% of ~96k customers ever buy twice. Distinct from
month-over-month retention (below); do not conflate.

**2. Month-over-month retention is very low (~≥99% of monthly actives buy 0 again the next month).**
E-commerce context means multi-month gaps may be normal; treat relative to benchmarks.

**3. Late delivery is strongly associated with lower reviews** (4.29 vs 2.27; diff 2.02 pts,
95% CI [1.98, 2.06], Cohen's d 1.47, Welch p<0.001). Observational — not causal.

**4. Revenue concentrated:** SP ≈ R$6M of R$16M; top categories: bed/bath, health & beauty,
computers accessories (item-grain revenue).

**Recommendations**
- Retention program post-purchase (30/60/90-day re-engagement).
- Prioritize carrier SLAs in late-delivery hotspots; investigate geo/category confounders first.
- Over-index marketing + logistics on SP and top categories.
